Balikbayan Protocol — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Short version: Balikbayan Protocol doesn't collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Balikbayan Protocol asks for one piece of information — your date of birth — so it can bound how far back in the app's timeline you're allowed to "jump." It also keeps a small count of how many days you've opened the app, and a one-hour session timer.

All of this is saved only in your device's local app storage (the same mechanism any app uses to remember your settings). None of it is sent to a server, because the app doesn't have one. Uninstalling the app deletes this data along with it. The in-app RESET button clears the day counter at any time.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or personal profile of any kind.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera — the app never requests those permissions.
  • No data is shared with or sold to any third party, because none is collected in the first place.

Network access

The app requests the standard Android internet permission for exactly one purpose: loading three typeface files from Google Fonts over HTTPS so the interface displays correctly. That request contains no personal information — it's the same kind of request your phone's browser makes to load a web font. See Google's privacy policy for how Google Fonts handles font requests generally.

Historical content

The Philippine history facts shown in the app (politics, entertainment, technology, and cultural trends by era) are a fixed, curated reference compiled ahead of time and bundled into the app. Looking up a date does not send any request anywhere or reveal anything about you.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children, so there is nothing to safeguard beyond what's described above.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
